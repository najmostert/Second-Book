# Review structure vs plan

## Read first

- `story planning/ideas-and-outline.md`
- `story planning/life-timeline.md` (if used)
- `current manuscript/00-table-of-contents.md`
- Relevant chapter files

## Report in plain language

1. **How the book is ordered now** (short)
2. **Match to the plan** — aligned / missing / out of order
3. **Gaps** — life periods with diary but no chapter (or vice versa)
4. **Suggestions** — at most 3, as options she can choose

## Rules

- Do not reorder or rename chapters without her say-so.
- If you propose a new chapter, describe it first; create only when she agrees.
- Never invent missing life events to “fill” the structure.
