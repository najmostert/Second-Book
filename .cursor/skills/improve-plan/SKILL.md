# Improve plan

## Files

- `story planning/ideas-and-outline.md` — main spine
- `story planning/life-timeline.md` — dates/places
- `story planning/where-we-left-off.md` — session continuity

## Process

1. Ask (or infer) what she wants: clearer periods, better chapter order, themes, or people list.
2. Propose changes in everyday words **before** rewriting large sections of the plan.
3. Update the plan files only after she agrees (or when she clearly said “go ahead”).
4. Explain what you changed and that the **chapters themselves were not rewritten** unless she asked.

## Truthfulness

- Plans organise real life; they must not invent years, places, or events.
- Mark uncertainties as questions, not facts.
- Link to diary dates when known.
