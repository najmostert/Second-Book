# Undo and reassure

## Priority

Her trust > cleverness. Act quickly and calmly.

## If she says “undo that” / “put it back”

1. Restore the **immediately previous** version of the text you changed this turn (or the change she points to).
2. Confirm: “I’ve put your earlier wording back in [chapter].”
3. Do not lecture; offer to try a lighter edit if she still wants help.

## If she says “I don’t like this version”

Offer two choices in plain language:

- Put my old words back
- Try a gentler edit

Then wait for her pick if unclear.

## If she asks what changed

Give a short bullet list in everyday words — no file paths jargon, no git.

## If she wants an older day

Say you can help restore from recent saved copies and do it for her. Do not ask her to use a terminal. If you need Jan for a deep restore, say so gently: “I’ll need Jan’s help for a deeper restore — your latest writing is still safe on this computer.”
