# Check prose

## Default mode: advise first

1. Read the section.
2. Give 3–6 friendly observations (what sounds like her; where it snags).
3. Offer: “Would you like me to apply a light polish?” — wait if unclear.

## If she wants changes applied

- Keep her voice; avoid generic “AI memoir” tone.
- Prefer shorter sentences when breathless; keep longer ones when reflective and clear.
- Never invent sensory detail that isn’t implied by her text or diary.
- Summarise what changed; remind her she can say **undo that**.

## Voice guardrails

- First person is normal for this autobiography.
- Emotional honesty over literary flourish.
- If a passage feels too polished vs her diary, say so and offer a closer-to-diary version.
