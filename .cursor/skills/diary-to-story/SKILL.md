# Diary to story

## Two kinds of pages

| Kind | Folder | Role |
|------|--------|------|
| Diary | `diary enteries/` | Source — her real notes |
| Chapter | `current manuscript/` | Shaped book prose |

Never overwrite a diary file with a chapter draft. Create or update a **chapter** instead.

## Create a diary entry

1. Make `diary enteries/YYYY-MM-DD.md` with a clear title (date) and blank space to write.
2. Tell her the page is ready and she can type freely (Word-like editor).

## Diary → chapter draft

1. Read the diary entry(ies) she points to.
2. Say what you will create (new chapter vs add to existing).
3. Draft in first person, staying faithful to the diary.
4. Where the diary is silent, leave a gentle placeholder or ask — do not invent.
5. Update the table of contents if a new chapter was added.
6. Comfort: “Your diary page is unchanged. The chapter is a separate draft. Say **undo that** to remove the draft changes.”

## Match diaries to a chapter

List candidate diary dates/themes that fit; let her choose before drafting.
