# Edit and polish

## Before editing

1. Confirm which chapter and roughly which part (or use the open / selected text).
2. Say: “I’ll gently polish this section in [chapter]. Your meaning stays the same. Say **undo that** if you dislike it.”
3. Do **not** invent events, feelings she didn’t write, or new people.

## How to edit

Follow **mum-voice** (confirm first). Prefer small changes; no mass rewrite (see **current-goal**). Keep facts and her phrasing.

**Defaults for every edit:** past-tense narrative, British English spelling (e.g. **towards** not *toward*), light grammar. Do not switch to present tense or American spelling unless she asks.

Summarise changes in plain bullets; offer **undo that**.


## Ask first when

- Changing chronology or names
- Cutting more than a short passage
- Merging two scenes that change meaning

## After

Update `where-we-left-off` if this was the main work. Run backup script.
