# Review chapter

## Process

1. Open/read the chapter and glance at the TOC for context.
2. Optionally skim related diary notes or the life outline if they exist.
3. Give a warm review — not a school mark.

## Review shape

- **What’s working** (2–4 points)
- **Where a reader might stumble** (2–4 points)
- **Small next steps** (up to 3, optional)

Do **not** rewrite the chapter during a review unless she asks. Offer: “I can polish X if you want.”

## Continuity

If dates, places, or people conflict with another chapter or the timeline, mention it gently and ask which is correct — never silent-correct life facts.
