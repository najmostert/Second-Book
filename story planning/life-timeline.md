## Questions to settle

1. **Which year was the Victoria Falls reunion?** No year given anywhere in the chapter.
2. **Which year was Christmas in Kenya?** Only "the 8th" for the flight home.
3. **Which year was the Shire Highlands trip in Malawi?** It says "the next year" — probably 2011, but the chapter never says.
4. **Which year was the Sweden trip with the customs man?** After the 2002 wedding, and it runs into Finland in 2008.
5. **Was Tim 32 or 33?** He turns 32 in Japan 2025, then is 33 for a discount at Dunluce a few months later.
6. **The Uganda years** — Mum says almost eleven, Jan says ten. Probably counting from different points.
7. **John's death** — the Venice chapter says "a month ago" and "eight years later" in one passage.
8. **Was Madagascar 2003 in September?** Dan's email is dated 23 September; the rest of the month isn't dated.
