# Threads and echoes

The things that come back. A book feels whole rather than like a stack of holidays when a few things recur and quietly change meaning.

Everything here is already in the pages. The job is to notice it, not to add it.

**Marked ○ = a thread that starts strongly and then goes quiet. These are the opportunities.**

---

## ○ "Where are you from?" — the spine

The question is asked outright in the preface, and then, remarkably, survives for the next fifty chapters almost entirely as a joke about her name being got wrong.

**The name gag** (this is the thread, and it's a good one):

| Version | Where |
|---|---|
| Mosteots · Mostepots · Mestart · Masstert · "Coolin Mestart" | Kenya 1992, Malawi 1996, India 2000 |
| "Coleen" | Madagascar 2003 |
| 'Timot V R Mos' and 'Janeth' | Istanbul 2017 |
| "Dora D'Oraborous" — the Facebook prank she can't undo | Croatia 2017, Venice 2017, Serbia 2018 |
| *"'You Jenna?' 'Um, I'm Jan.'"* | Bali 2023 |
| *"'Welcome to Ireland Janette'* in a glorious Dublin accent" | Ireland 2025 |

Fifty years of nobody quite getting her name right, ending with an Irishman getting it right. **That is the whole book in one running joke and it happened by accident.**

**The direct hits:**

- *"People often ask me, 'Where are you from?'"* — preface
- *"GO HOME BRITISH PIGS!"* — Uganda ch. 2 and again in pink paint, Cape Town ch. 3
- *"You people make me sick!"* — the MP, ch. 2
- *"I think I have just met apartheid in reverse"* — Mum, ch. 2
- *"She asked where I was from and I waved the bog roll in the general direction and said, 'South Africa'."* — Madagascar 2003
- *"Being partially British, (hmm will this statement direct me later?)"* — Bali 2023 ← **she is asking her own book**
- *"British reserve"* — Thailand 2022, walking 4.5km rather than ask for a lift
- *"typical noisy gaijin"* — Japan 2025
- *"It's a bit weird"* — when Tim asks what it's like being back in Ireland, 2019

**Accents:** she can do British, Derry, Dublin, Scottish, Indian, drunk bergie, and teach anatomy in Afrikaans. Col does everything in *"his distinctive plummy 'Rhodesian' accent."*

## ○ Mum, and the pink Mac

The reason the book exists. Then almost silent for forty chapters.

- *"I asked my mother to write about her experiences… She used an old pink Mac"* — preface
- Her whole Uganda memoir, in her own voice — ch. 2
- *"My dear mum died in 2014"* — ch. 2
- Barely present through the middle of the book
- *"still reeling from the untimely death of my dear mum"* — Italy 2014
- **The payoff, unset-up:** Bali 2023, on her birthday — *"We order three Bintang beers and toast Mum… 'one for Mum'… 'She'll have cheesecake with cinnamon ice cream.' Because she would have."*

That last line is among the best in the book. It arrives from nowhere. **A very small amount of preparation would make it devastating.**

## Tim: supplement → addition → the one she travels with

The clearest arc in the book, and it's already perfect.

1. *"Tim, as the unofficial 'supplement', didn't get a plate"* — Madagascar 2003
2. *"He was nearly as tall as me now"* — Madagascar 2006
3. *"Instead of merely being the quiet 'supplement'… he has officially moved up to full 'addition' status"* — Victoria Falls
4. *"Tim has completed high school and is taking a gap year"* — Japan 2012
5. *"Tim was beginning his life as a digital nomad"* — Norway 2018
6. From here he books the trips, carries the bags, and plays her a poem — Ireland 2025
7. *"'Get off quickly mother!'"* · *"'Sláinte mother'"* — the way he says *mother* is a thread in itself
8. Marina, his girlfriend — Japan 2025

## Undignified places to sit, sleep, and squat

The great comic seam of the book. Nearly every chapter has one.

Hiding in the school toilets with a book · the long drop at the border · the crochet-covered loo · the coconut laxative queue · the phantom floater · sawdust outdoor toilet in Sweden · **the frozen toilet seat that "attached itself to his nether regions"** · long drop with the candle blowing out · squatting behind fireplaces, gravel in Col's face · **the camera bag pillow** · the "inflatable skirt" at the Ethiopian roadside · Hotel Hovel with the human turd on the shower grid · Tim's face between the bed planks · beds of ice in Lapland · capsule hotel, foot to head · the sleeping pod at Doha · the Amed toilet on a pedestal · *"lie in an undignified heap"* · tea ceremony, *"legs splayed unbecomingly"* · **the twelve-inch sponge mattress in Ireland, and "positions that shouldn't be attempted by anyone after forty or 'farty' as they say here."**

Note how it changes: as a child she hides in a toilet to be alone. Sixty years later she's laughing about getting off a sponge mattress. **Same joke, different woman.**

## Animals

Col's birds and butterflies drive twenty years of travel. Lemurs, tigers, jaguars, geladas, an Ethiopian wolf, Cecil the lion.

Then the animals change too: **Fish and Chips** the cats, Shadow the dog, Hachikō the loyal dog statue — visited in 2019 and again in 2025. She goes from hunting rare animals across continents to missing her kitties.

## How to use this

- A thread pays off when it **returns changed**, not when it's repeated.
- Never point at it. If a reader notices we planted something, it's ruined.
- Adding a thread means finding a real memory, never inventing one.
- The four ○ threads are the honest opportunities: the question, Mum, Dad and Ireland, and the shift in who she travels as.
