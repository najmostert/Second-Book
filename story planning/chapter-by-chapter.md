## What the lengths show

- **Six chapters carry a third of the book:** Uganda (18k), Jakarta/Bali (20k), Japan 2025 (15k), Madagascar 2003 (13k), India 2000 (12k), India 2001 (11k).
- **Eight chapters are under a thousand words.** Some are fine as short breathers. Two are not: **Saudi Arabia — three years of her life in 746 words**, and **Namibia as a teenager in 312**.
- **Barcelona at 335 words** reads as a fragment rather than a chapter. It could join Paris.
- The whole Saudi period is skipped and then described entirely through two Indian holidays taken to get away from it. That may be deliberate. Worth asking.
