## Three things that will confuse a reader (and us)

**1. Two different Johns.**
Her brother John (wheelchair, artist, Clarens) — and John, the host in Malawi with Fumiyo. Completely different men. Any note or edit must say which.

**2. John and Jonny may be the same person.**
The brother's death is written in the Venice chapter (around 2017). Then in Singapore 2024 she writes about "Jonny's birthday" and the anniversary of his death. Almost certainly the same brother — but the book never says so. **Question for her: shall we settle on one name?**

**3. Ivan appears twice and isn't the same man.**
Her father Ivan Crowley — and Ivan, a hostel worker in Split in the Venice chapter. Coincidence, but worth watching.

## A date that needs her eye

In the Venice chapter, in one passage, she writes that her brother *"died in unfortunate circumstances a month ago"* and, a few lines later, *"Eight years later, I am still waiting for an apology from the hospital."*

Both can't sit in the same paragraph as written. She may have added the second line years later while writing the book — which would make sense. **Ask her; don't fix it.**

## Countries she has lived in

The preface says **six**. From the chapters: Uganda, England, Ireland, South Africa, South West Africa/Namibia, Saudi Arabia. That makes six if Ireland counts as living rather than boarding school.

**Question for her: which six did she mean?** Worth being sure, since it's the second sentence of the book.
