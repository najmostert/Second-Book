# Writing in this project

You write in **Markdown** — plain text with a few simple symbols for formatting. Cursor shows a nice preview if you open the preview pane (optional).

## Daily workflow

1. Open **`current manuscript/`** and your latest chapter.
2. Write. Save with **Cmd + S** when you want.
3. Ask the AI for help anytime — editing, ideas, new chapters, diary entries.

## Chapters

- Each chapter is its own file: `01-chapter-one.md`, `02-next-chapter.md`, etc.
- **`00-table-of-contents.md`** lists all chapters — ask the AI to update it when you add one.

## Diary entries

- One file per day in **`diary enteries/`**, named like `2026-07-24.md`.
- Say “create today's diary entry” and the AI will set it up.

## Planning

Use **`story planning/ideas-and-outline.md`** for characters, plot, and notes that aren't part of the manuscript yet.

## Voice & style

The AI is instructed to **keep your voice** — it fixes clarity and grammar but won't rewrite everything unless you ask. If an edit feels wrong, say “undo that” or “keep more of my original wording.”

## Old drafts

Your previous work in Apple Pages lives in **`legacy/`**. To reuse text, open the Pages file, copy what you need, and paste into chat or a chapter file — the AI can help tidy it up.
